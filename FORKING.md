## Fork identity and credit

Use a distinct product name and replace the official logos, icons, favicons, and in-app
branding in a public fork. See [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md). You may identify the upstream
accurately in prose. We ask forks to include **“Based on Brown AI by Vedant Wankhade”** in
their README and About or credits screen, and to summarize their changes. This credit and the
request to make improvements are community guidance, not extra AGPL conditions.

AGPL-3.0-only requires source sharing in the circumstances it describes; it does not require
publishing unrelated product source code, private modifications never conveyed or offered
over a network, or an entire organization's codebase. Keep third-party license obligations
separate.
