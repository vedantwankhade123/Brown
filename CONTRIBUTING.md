Thanks for considering a contribution to Brown AI. By submitting a contribution, you confirm
that you have the right to submit it and agree that your contribution may be distributed under
the GNU Affero General Public License, version 3 only (AGPL-3.0-only). You keep copyright in
your contribution; this project does not require a copyright assignment or CLA.

Please:

- Describe the problem and the behavior your change adds or fixes.
- Avoid including secrets, credentials, personal data, or assets you cannot redistribute.
- Keep third-party code and assets under their original licenses and include their notices.
- For changes to a fork, use your own product identity and preserve required upstream notices.
