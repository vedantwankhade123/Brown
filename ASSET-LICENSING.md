# Assets and third-party materials

The root `LICENSE` applies to Brown AI project-authored software source code, except where a
file or directory has its own license notice. It does not automatically relicense every
image, logo, icon, font, model, sound, video, dataset, or other bundled material.

## Brown AI brand assets

Official Brown AI and UseBrown logos, app icons, favicons, and other brand artwork are
reserved. They are not licensed for use as the identity of a fork. See [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md).
Fork maintainers should replace them with artwork they have the right to use.

## Third-party and generated materials

Third-party libraries and assets retain their own licenses and notices. Check their package
metadata and upstream license files before redistributing a build. Do not assume an AGPL
license on project code grants rights to model weights, hosted services, stock media, or
third-party marks. Generated build output, release binaries, and downloaded models may have
separate terms from their source code.

If a file has no clear license or ownership notice, do not assume this file grants additional
rights to that material; ask the maintainer or replace it with material you can license.
